Dictionary data in this folder: oewn-2025.json.gz

This is a TRIMMED COPY of the Open English WordNet 2025 (OEWN), made for SM Reader.
Only headwords, parts of speech, definitions, the original spelling where it differs
from the lower-case headword, and a list of irregular inflected forms were kept.
Examples, pronunciations, relations (hypernyms, antonyms, ...) and ids were removed.
